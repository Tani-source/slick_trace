# SlickTrace — Manual Task Assignments

> These are the **non-automatable** tasks. The AI agent handles all code. Your job is to get the right data, credentials, and environment into its hands.

> **DATA DECISION (made):** For the hackathon time budget, and because real data sources
> (Sentinel/CMEMS/ERA5/MarineCadastre) each require free accounts + licence acceptance
> (hours–days of waiting) and the SAR training archive alone is ~40–97 GB, the team chose a
> **fully synthetic, deterministic dataset** — generated, not downloaded, by
> `backend/scripts/generate_all.py`. It is clearly labelled synthetic (rules.md §3.1) and
> sized ~150 MB (well under budget). Regenerating reproduces byte-identical files. The
> real-data scripts (`download_sar_dataset.py`, `download_sentinel1.py`) are retained as a
> roadmap note but are NOT part of the demo path.
